    SUPER ORBITAL SIMULATOR

    Build and run a space fort for the war effort.

    Your assignment: the hulk of the SES Patient Fury. The Ministry has permitted its middle section. Fill it, connect it, and make it pay.

    1. Pick a module at the bottom, then click inside the yellow permitted section. Drag to lay corridors and lifts.
    2. Docking bays must touch open space: the top deck, the bottom deck, or the hull's ends.
    3. Everything must connect to Station Command. People walk along decks and only change deck in a lift.
    4. Ships dock, haulers carry the crates to a cargo hold, and every crate pays. Residents pay the Liberty Contribution.
    5. In BUDGET you fund the divisions. You set the money; the simulation decides the people.
