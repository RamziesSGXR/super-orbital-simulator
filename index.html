SUPER ORBITAL SIMULATOR

Your station. Its people. What happens to them.

  1. The station has a job. It is the fire support platform over a planet at war. Build the FIRE SUPPORT parts (a Ship Gun, Orbital Batteries, hangars, barracks) and keep them crewed and supplied. You build it; you never fire it.
  2. Lay it out. Pick a part at the bottom left and click near a port to attach it. Select a part and press EDIT INSIDE to set its rooms.
  3. Then it is theirs. Seven factions (Gunnery, Supply, Maintenance, Security, Medical, Command, Science) each want the station their way. They take empty space and set up what they need without asking.
  4. Only trouble reaches you. Two factions after the same room, an allotment the stores cannot cover, a call from the ground nobody answered. It lands on your desk or on comms. Supplies come by the crate; the score is the station's capability.

New station: Station Command, 2,500 CR and an Outpost permit. Broken station: take over a dark, stripped station and bring it back. Free play: every part and room, no costs, and controls to call in ships, colonists and raids. Prototype 0.11. You found your faction first: its name, crest and colours. The station saves itself as you play.
