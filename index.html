SUPER ORBITAL SIMULATOR

Build and run a space station for the war effort.

  1. Pick a part from a group at the bottom left, then click near a port to attach it: little blue dots show the free ports near the pointer. R turns it.
  2. Select a built part and press EDIT INSIDE to build out its rooms: quarters, mess halls, workshops, markets, shuttle bays and more. A part does what its rooms do.
  3. Fund your divisions and give them standing orders on the Commander's channel. Their reports come in on the comms at the bottom right.
  4. Climb the upgrade path from Outpost to Citadel for bigger build zones, hangars and drydocks. H hides the interface for a clean look.

Economy: start with Station Command and 2,500 CR, keep it profitable, and earn your way up the upgrade path. Free play: every part and room, no costs, the biggest build zone, and controls to call in ships and colonists. Prototype 0.3, nothing is saved yet.
