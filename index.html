SUPER ORBITAL SIMULATOR

Build and run a space station for the war effort.

  1. Pick a part from the grid at the bottom left, then click one of the glowing ports to attach it. R turns it.
  2. Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  3. Docking ports bring ships. Haulers carry their crates to a cargo module, and every crate pays. Habs and rings house colonists, who pay tax.
  4. Switch views at the bottom right: Blueprint, Hull, Life Signs, and a Cutaway that opens the station up.

Economy: start with Station Command and 2,000 CR, keep it profitable, and earn the Garrison Permit for the big parts. Free play: every part, no costs, a big build zone, and test tools for the simulation (spawn ships and colonists, switch traffic off, run up to 16×). Prototype 0.2, nothing is saved yet.
